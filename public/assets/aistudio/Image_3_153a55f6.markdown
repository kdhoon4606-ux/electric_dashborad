## Brand & Style
The design system targets energy market newcomers, retail prosumers, and commercial electricity monitors seeking accessible intelligence on wholesale system marginal price (SMP) movements. It balances analytical precision with a welcoming consumer-fintech sensibility. 

The aesthetic is Modern Clean with warm functional energy cues:
- **Clarity over Density:** Avoid wall-of-numbers fatigue typical in traditional energy trading terminals. Favor airy breathing room, scannable hierarchies, and plain-language interpretations.
- **Energized Utility:** The UI relies on a foundation of neutral slate surfaces illuminated by calibrated kinetic orange accents, symbolizing electric grid vitality without visual alarmism.
- **Approachable Rigor:** Mathematical exactness meets empathetic onboarding via conversational AI summaries, generous tap targets, and softened perimeter structures.

## Colors
The palette leverages a high-contrast, clean foundation paired with targeted energy accents:

- **Primary (`#EA580C`) & Tertiary (`#F97316`):** Reserved for active power states, peak pricing alerts, trend vectors, primary callouts, and interactive indicators. Tinted background fills utilize soft amber washes (`#FFEDD5` / `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)`).
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`):** The grounding slate anchor for primary headers, high-impact numerical indicators, and focal card structures.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Governs structural borders (`#E2E8F0`), secondary metadata labels (`#64748B`), and muted card track backgrounds (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Canvas Base:** Pristine canvas layering with `#F8FAFC` for page grounds and `#FFFFFF` for primary elevated cards.

## Typography
Typography is optimized for bilingual Korean-English numerical reading. 

- **Font Stack:** Leverages Inter integrated seamlessly with modern system sans-serif and Pretendard fallbacks for Korean character balance.
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric readouts, SMP rates (KRW/kWh), percentage changes, and timestamps must employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter across data updates.
- **Scale Hierarchy:** Metric readouts rely on high-impact weights (`font-weight: 700` and `800`) at tight line-heights, balanced with open, comfortable body line-heights (`1.55`–`1.6`) for narrative summaries.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid on desktop/tablet, collapsing to a single adaptive column on mobile.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width capped at 1280px. 12 columns with 1.5rem gutters and 2rem outer margins. Dashboard panels arrange into a 4-column stat row followed by an 8:4 split for the primary hourly bar chart versus the commentary card.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, stat metrics wrap to 2x2.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single column, 1rem edge padding. Stat cards transform into horizontal swipe snap reels or clean stacked keyframes with tightened vertical rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is achieved primarily through tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter lines rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Ground color `#F8FAFC`, flat.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, paired with an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Floating Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `border: 1px solid #CBD5E1` and ambient shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Peak Highlight Accentuation:** Bar charts or badge highlights utilize a subtle warm glow: `0 0 12px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.25)`.

## Shapes
The shape system balances modern analytical rigidity with soft, welcoming accessibility:

- **Micro elements (Badges, Chips, Inner Bars):** `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px) to maintain sharpness at small scales.
- **Form controls & Inputs:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Core Cards & Chart Canvas Containers:** `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded-2xl` (1.25rem / 20px) on large viewport cards to deliver a consumer-friendly feel.
- **Pills:** Used strictly for live status pips (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Metric Stat Cards
- Enclosed in `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-xl` and 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Padding: `space-lg`.
- Top row: Category tag (`label-md`, `#64748B`) paired with trend badge (soft orange pill `#FFEDD5` with `#EA580C` text).
- Main numeric readout (`stat-metric`, `#0F172A`) with trailing unit baseline (`KRW/kWh` in `#64748B`).
- Subtext displays 24-hour delta comparison.

### Hourly Bar Chart Container
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-2xl`, with interior padding `space-xl`.
- Header houses the chart title, real-time update indicator, and segmented view toggles.
- Baseline grid lines use `#F1F5F9`. Default hourly bars utilize neutral slate `#CBD5E1`.
- Energy Peak bars automatically switch to `#EA580C` with an optional top marker badge and warm glow.
- Hover/active tooltips present tabular SMP metrics, fuel source mix, and historical diffs.

### Date Picker & Filter Dropdowns
- Compact trigger button (`rounded-lg`, border `#E2E8F0`, bg `#FFFFFF`, text `#334155`).
- Active/Open state adopts `#EA580C` border outline with `0 0 0 3px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)`.
- Flyout menu follows Level 2 elevation with smooth entry transition.

### Market Commentary Card
- Container: Soft warm gradient tint (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFF7ED 0%, #FFFFFF 100%)`) with border tint `#FED7AA`.
- Iconography: Friendly energy spark/lightbulb glyph housed in an orange badge (`#EA580C` icon on `#FFEDD5`).
- Body: Conversational market overview translated into concise, beginner-friendly Korean bullet items.

### Source Disclaimer Footer
- Minimal, clean typographic block styled in `body-md` / `#64748B` with `border-t: 1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Lists official power exchange data attribution (KPX / 한국전력거래소), refresh intervals, and statutory disclaimers.
